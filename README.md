# lemontron

Builder of useful systems, strange machines, and small tools that punch above their weight.

I like software that is direct, hardware that tells the truth, and interfaces that stay out of the way. Most of my work lives somewhere between product engineering, automation, fabrication, and the occasional idea that looked simple until it met reality.

## What I work on

- Practical web apps and developer tools
- 3D printing, electronics, and shop-adjacent automation
- Local-first workflows and small services
- Experiments that turn into repositories before they turn into explanations

## Current themes

- Making personal infrastructure easier to operate
- Turning recurring manual tasks into boring software
- Building tools that feel fast, legible, and durable

## Around here

You will find a mix of polished projects, active experiments, and notes from the workshop floor. If something looks useful, take it apart, improve it, or send a note.
